Hearvia

Live subtitles for anything you hear, in your language.

Hearvia listens to other apps or the microphone and shows translated subtitles on top of whatever you're doing, on iPhone and Mac.

Coming soon to TestFlight iPhone and Mac · iOS 27 and macOS 27

Hears other apps

On iPhone, a floating window shows subtitles over videos, podcasts and calls. On Mac, an overlay follows all system audio or just one app.

Never gets in the way

Hearvia doesn't play sound or pause anything. What you're watching keeps playing.

Your choice of translator

Free on-device translation that works offline, an experimental local model you run yourself, or OpenAI Realtime and Gemini Live with your own API key.

Private by default

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Transcripts stay on your device, or in your own iCloud if you turn on sync.
